Steering Committee

Meet the Team

We’re all parents and carers of children with SEND in Slough. This is who’s currently steering the forum, but the real work happens across every voice we listen to.

🤝

We are just like you.

Every member of our steering committee is a parent or carer of a child with special educational needs and disabilities. We understand the journey because we are living it too.
