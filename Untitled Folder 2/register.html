Join the Forum

Help make a difference for families in Slough with Slough’s official SEND parent carer forum.

Why you should join:

Share your experiences and views
Receive SEND-related news, events and surveys
Contribute to shaping local SEND policy and services with the official forum

Who can join?

Membership is open to parents, carers and family members of children and young people aged 0–25 with special educational needs or disabilities who live in or access services in Slough.
