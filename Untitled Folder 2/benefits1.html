Guidance & Help

What You Might Be Entitled To

Raising a child with SEND comes with real extra costs like therapies, equipment, lost income and more. There’s more financial help available than most families realise. Here’s an overview of what to look into.

⚠️ This is not an exhaustive list and information may change. Always check the official sources before applying.

For your child

Benefits paid to support a disabled child or young person.

Under 16

Disability Living Allowance (DLA)

A tax-free benefit for children under 16 who need more care, supervision or help getting around than other children their age. No diagnosis required. It is based on the support your child actually needs. Not means-tested. Rates range from around £30 to £195 a week (2026/27).

How to apply on Gov.uk
16 and over

Personal Independence Payment (PIP)

Replaces DLA when your child turns 16. They’ll be invited to apply by the DWP, and it’s a fresh assessment, so a previous DLA award doesn’t guarantee PIP. Worth getting help from SENDIASS or Citizens Advice for the form.

PIP on Gov.uk
Higher rate mobility

Motability Scheme

If your child gets the higher rate mobility component of DLA (from age 3) or the enhanced rate of PIP, you can use that money to lease a car, powered wheelchair or scooter through Motability.

Motability Scheme

For you as a carer

Money and protections for the person doing the caring.

35+ hours/week

Carer's Allowance

£86.45 a week (2026/27) if you spend at least 35 hours a week caring for someone who gets DLA middle/highest care rate, PIP daily living, or Attendance Allowance. There’s an earnings limit, so check before applying if you work.

Carer's Allowance on Gov.uk
National Insurance

Carer's Credit

If you can’t claim Carer’s Allowance (often because you earn too much), Carer’s Credit fills gaps in your National Insurance record so your State Pension isn’t reduced for the years you spent caring.

Carer's Credit on Gov.uk
Often missed

Universal Credit (carer & disabled child elements)

If you’re on UC, there’s a carer element for caring 35+ hours a week, and a disabled child element paid on top of your standard UC if your child gets DLA or PIP. The disabled child element alone can be over £160 a month, more if the child is severely disabled. Update your UC journal as soon as DLA is awarded.

Universal Credit on Gov.uk

Grants & one-off help

Charity grants for equipment, breaks, or specific costs.

The big one

Family Fund

The UK’s largest grants programme for low-income families raising disabled or seriously ill children (0-17). Funds things like sensory equipment, white goods, family breaks, tablets and clothing. No diagnosis needed, and eligibility is based on care needs across at least three areas. Opens every 24 months.

Family Fund
Equipment

Newlife Foundation

Grants for specialist equipment, specialist chairs, beds, communication aids, that the NHS or council won’t fund. They also run an emergency loan service for terminally ill children.

Newlife
Brain conditions

Cerebra

For children with conditions affecting the brain. Free sleep service, a lending library of toys and equipment, a legal entitlements service, and a parent guide library covering everything from EHCPs to challenging behaviour.

Cerebra
Equipment & therapy

Caudwell Children

Grants for specialist equipment, treatment (including autism assessments) and family support, for children under 18 with a disability or serious illness in households earning under £45k.

Caudwell Children

Everyday savings

Reductions and exemptions that quietly add up.

Often missed

Council tax disability reduction

If your home has been adapted for a disabled person, extra space for a wheelchair, a downstairs bathroom or bedroom because of disability, your council tax can drop by one band. Apply through Slough Borough Council, and savings can be backdated.

Council tax discounts - Slough Council
Parking

Blue Badge

A disabled parking permit. Automatic if your child gets the higher rate DLA mobility component. Otherwise apply through Slough Borough Council, non-physical disabilities such as autism and cognitive conditions can qualify too.

Apply for a Blue Badge
Home adaptations

Disabled Facilities Grant (DFG)

Up to £30,000 from your council towards home adaptations, ramps, stairlifts, ground-floor bathrooms, widened doorways. Means-tested for adults, but not means-tested when it’s for a disabled child. Apply through Slough Borough Council.

DFG on Gov.uk
SEND

Free school transport

If your child can’t reasonably walk to their nearest suitable school because of SEND, the council must arrange free transport. Eligibility runs through Slough’s Local Offer.

Slough Local Offer
Tax exemption

VAT relief on disability equipment

When buying things designed for a disabled person, adapted clothing, sensory equipment, certain mobility aids, you may not have to pay VAT. Just tick the eligibility declaration at checkout.

VAT relief on Gov.uk
