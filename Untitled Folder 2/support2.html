Slough Information, Advice and Support Service

Slough’s SEND IASS provides:

  • › Free, impartial advice for parents and carers
  • › Help understanding SEND law and processes
  • › Support preparing for meetings or appeals
Visit Your SEND IASS

The Autism Group

Based in Maidenhead, they provide:

  • › Special interest clubs for autistic children and young people aged 9–25, held on Saturdays in central Maidenhead
  • › Free TAG talks and workshops giving parents insight and simple strategies to support their young person
  • › Parent support through coffee and chat sessions across East Berkshire, plus 1-to-1 help via TAG@Home
Find out more

Autism Berkshire

Founded in 1990, Berkshire Autistic Society is the leading autism charity in the area, delivering training, specialist services, and leisure activities.

Find out more

Schools and SENCos

For many families, the first step is:

  • › Speaking to the school special educational needs coordinator (SENCo)
  • › Discussing concerns with teachers
  • › Requesting meetings to review support

Schools should be able to guide parents towards additional services where needed.

Slough Children First

Owned wholly by Slough Borough Council, Slough Children First provides social care and support services to children, young people and families.

General SEND Support Services

IPSEA

The Independent Provider of Special Education Advice offers free, independent legal advice on SEND law, EHCPs, appeals and tribunals.

  • • Expert advice on your rights
  • • Tribunal support
  • • Template letters & guides
  • • Helpline & email advice
Get Legal Advice

Contact

Contact for families with disabled children is a leading charity for families raising disabled children.

  • • Free helpline & listening ear service
  • • Parent-to-parent support
  • • Practical guides & financial help
  • • Local groups & events
Visit Contact
Scope

Scope

Scope is a disability equality charity offering both practical and emotional support.

  • • Information on rights & benefits
  • • Education & employment advice
  • • Campaigning for better inclusion
  • • Parent support services
Visit Scope

YoungMinds

YoungMinds is a UK charity fighting for children and young people’s mental health, with parent guides and a helpline.

  • • Advice on anxiety, school refusal & emotional wellbeing
  • • Parent guides & helpline
  • • Crisis support information
Visit YoungMinds

SOS!SEN

SOS!SEN is an independent national helpline for special education advice.

  • • Free confidential telephone support
  • • Help securing the right educational provision
  • • Advice on SEN processes
Visit SOS!SEN

Family Fund

Family Fund is a UK charity working with government and various SEND services to support families in various ways.

  • • Grants for low income households
  • • Free virtual workshops online
  • • Advice and information on SEND services and financing guidance
Visit Family Fund
