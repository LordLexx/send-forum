SEND Café Monthly Drop-in

10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

The Curve, Slough Library

About this Event

Grab a coffee and chat with other parent carers in a relaxed, understanding environment. No agenda, just peer support. Being a parent carer can be isolating, and sometimes you just need to talk to someone who "gets it".

Our monthly café is open to all parent carers of children and young people with Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND) in Slough. Whether your child has a diagnosis, is on a waiting list, or you simply have concerns about their development, you are welcome here.

Our friendly staff and volunteers will be on hand to answer questions, signpost to local services, or simply top up your teacup. We occasionally invite local professionals to drop in informally, so you can ask questions in a low-pressure setting.

What to Expect

  • 1

    Arrival & Settling In

    Tea, coffee, and biscuits available. Find a seat and introduce yourself.

  • 2

    Open Chat & Support

    Informal discussions. Share your experiences or just listen to others.

  • 3

    Guest Speaker (Occasional)

    Sometimes we have a local professional drop in for 15 mins to share updates.
