What is a Parent Carer Forum?

We are an independent group of parents and carers of children with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND). We work together with local services to improve support for our families.

What We Do

  • ✔ Represent your voice to those who plan SEND services
  • ✔ Signpost you to local and national support
  • ✔ Run events, workshops and drop-ins for parent carers
  • ✔ Gather feedback to help shape policy and strategy
