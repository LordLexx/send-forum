Tips for great newsletters

  • Press / in an empty block to quickly insert content.
  • Hover over any block and use the floating toolbar to drag, duplicate or delete it.
  • Highlight text inside a block to reveal the formatting toolbar (Bold, Italic, Link).
  • Use headings to break up long content — accessibility matters!
  • Switch to Style above to customise colours, fonts, spacing, header & footer.
