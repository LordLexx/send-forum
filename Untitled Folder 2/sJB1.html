SEND Jargon Buster

Demystifying terms and phrases you will hear on your SEND carer journey.

...

AAC

Augmentative and Alternative Communication

Methods of communication used to replace or supplement speech for those with impairments.

Additional Needs

A term used to describe children who have learning needs that require extra support.

ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder

A condition that affects people's behaviour. People with ADHD can seem restless, may have trouble concentrating and may act on impulse.

Annual Review

EHCP Review

The legal process of reviewing an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) at least once a year to ensure it remains accurate.

ASD

Autism Spectrum Disorder

A lifelong developmental disability that affects how a person communicates with and relates to other people, and how they experience the world.

CAF

Common Assessment Framework

A standardised approach to assessing the needs of children and young people, used to be replaced by EHC needs assessments.
